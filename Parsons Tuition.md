#### Maintenance of Status (check with program)
-  Some colleges or schools require degree students to maintain continuous residency. Students who are neither registering to take courses for credit nor on approved leave of absence must register to maintain status and are charged the Maintenance of Status fee. Typically, graduate students working on a thesis or dissertation.
