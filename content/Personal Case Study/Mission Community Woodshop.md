Mission Community Woodshop is a wood working studio based in inner mission of San Francisco. Run by two former teachers, Andrew and Daniel, the studio aims to teach the craft of woodworking and provide workspaces for artisans to utilize.

The studio approached me with the goal of creating new streams of revenue to expand on their current offering and support a residency program. We discussed building a fundraising program, digital assets to support outreach, updating their web platform and creating a blueprint for improving their digital footprint.
