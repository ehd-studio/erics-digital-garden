### Jennie C Jones on Lineage, time as teacher and reflection

[Time Sensitive Episode](https://www.timesensitive.fm/episode/jennie-c-jones-on-time-traveling-through-art-sound-and-space/](https://www.timesensitive.fm/episode/jennie-c-jones-on-time-traveling-through-art-sound-and-space/)
[Jennie C Jones Projects](https://www.clarkart.edu/microsites/ground-work/about-the-projects/jennie-c-jones](https://www.clarkart.edu/microsites/ground-work/about-the-projects/jennie-c-jones)

> “Listen, you’re going to find your spirit animals. You’re going to find your compadres in history, over time. You’re not going to be a unicorn that invents something. It’s not going to happen. You need to bolster up your ideas with kindred spirits, over time.”

> Often the thread is only this radicality, right? That way of time traveling, because it’s delightful, and interesting. It’s also maybe just to get the courage to say, “Well, fuck everything I’m doing, because look at how _they_ shifted things.” And maybe it was just a personal shift enough to find that courage, to depart from what your peers are doing, or what you feel like you should be doing.

- Read, experience, reflect and find others that you look up to. Dive into them and research the why
