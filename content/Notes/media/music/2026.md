### Pantheon
I'm sick of the Spotify Wrapped being wrong about what I listened to!
The pantheon is a way to organize what I've been listening to lately and have a dedicated list to what music in my eyes is all killer no filler or garbage. This is PURELY my opinion and based on my personal taste. Tiers are organized as such:
- Tier 1 - Top 10
- Tier 2 - Defining My Personality
- Tier 3 - Constant Rotation
- Tier 4 - I'll be excited when I rediscover this later this month
- Tier 5 - Seasonal Listens
