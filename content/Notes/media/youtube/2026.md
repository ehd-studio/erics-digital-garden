## Haven't Seen Yet (but will)
"Fishing With John" - Jazz man John Lurie simply goes fishing with some of his pals. Featuring Jim Jarmusch, Tom Waits, Matt Dillon, Willem Dafoe and Dennis Hopper. My second favourite fishing show to Rex Hunt’s Fishing Adventures. 

"Painting With John" - I can’t mention one without the other.   
Another strangely charming creation by professional weirdo John Lurie.   
  
"Martijn Doolaard" - A YT series about a Dutchman rebuilding a cabin in the Italian Alps. A weekly antidote to my domesticated yuppie condition. Perfectly slow.
