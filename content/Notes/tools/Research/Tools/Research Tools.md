## Methods

- _cross-platform_: terminologies/language used across platforms you use
- _cross-discipline_: how a topic is approached from different areas of study (eg. physics from a Black studies lens)
- _cross-genre:_ how an area of study is approached from different mediums outside of academic writing (poetry, music, art)
- _inquiry:_ practice asking questions and speculation through discussion or writing
- _citational lineage:_ move through study by looking at works cited on pieces of information you want to look into
