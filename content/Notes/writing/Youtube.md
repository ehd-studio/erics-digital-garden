Who? That’s the creator and showrunner (real name **Cooper Smith Goodwin**) behind _The Amazing Digital Circus_, the latest YouTube-incubated phenomenon to hit big in theaters. _Crazy fact_: *The Amazing Digital Circus* along with _Obsession_ and _Backrooms_, the so-called “YouTuber movies” have generated more than $300 million domestic in the past month, per Screendollars.
