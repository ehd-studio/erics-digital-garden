*Smol* is an application designed to connect fashion influencers with local artisans around the world. The platform gives influencers a way to discover shops showcasing growing local brands, and to feature them in their content, while giving artisans another way to advertise their brand to a high impact audience. By bridging creators and taste makers across different regions, *Smol* aims to make smaller retail taste makers more visible and offer followers a network of brands across different metropolitan centers around the world.
