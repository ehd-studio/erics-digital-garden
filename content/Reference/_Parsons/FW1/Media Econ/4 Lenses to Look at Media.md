## 1. Company
### How do companies make decisions?

- Fastest way to assess what's happening with a company is to look at the income statement
	- Revenues - Expenses = Operating Income
- To understand the challenges and opportunities a company is facing you need to look at long term financial trends
- How is this company financially incentivized to make content?
	- [[Blockbuster Economics]]
	- Are there more incentives to take less big bets?
- Does the company own its own content?
- How much does its library value drive its earnings?
- How is the marketing funnel for the company changing?
- Where is the company looking for new revenue streams (eg. fandoms)?
