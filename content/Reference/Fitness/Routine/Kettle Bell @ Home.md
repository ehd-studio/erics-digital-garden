**Warm-up (5-10 minutes):** Start with light cardio like star jumps or marching on the spot, followed by dynamic stretches such as arm swings and hip circles. Alternatively, you can use a light kettlebell to do mobility exercises like halos and slingshots.

**The Workout:**

- **Kettlebell Squat:** 3 sets of 8 reps
- **Kettlebell Swing:** 3 sets of 8 reps
- **Kettlebell Row:** 3 sets of 8 reps
- **Kettlebell Overhead Press:** 3 sets of 8 reps
- **Turkish Get-Up:** 3 sets of 6 reps on each side

_To make this a 3-day-a-week program, you can swap the exercises on different days with variations like Kettlebell Romanian Deadlifts, Kettlebell Snatches, and Lateral Lunges_.
