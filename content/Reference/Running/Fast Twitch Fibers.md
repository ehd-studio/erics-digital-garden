*Fast Twitch Fibers* (or Type II Fibers) are the larger skeletal muscle cells that are meant for explosive bursts of speed rather than long endurance.

---
### Characteristics
- Wider in diameter
- Produce anaerobically (for the most part)
	- they instead rely on ATP, high energy molecules for energy
- Use of ATP instead of steady oxygen flow makes them tire quicker
- Contain two subgroups of 11a and 11b muscle fibers (one of which shares similar characteristics to slow twitch)
