*Slow twitch fibers* (also called Type 1) are skeletal muscle cells designed for endurance and low force-contractions.

---
Type 1 Fibers are key to endurance performance because of their efficient use of oxygen as fuel and their resistance to fatigue.
### Characteristics
- Slower shortening speed - cannot generate as much force, but are steady over time
- Aerobic fibers - use oxygen to transfer energy
- Large capillary area
- Allow for aerobic metabolism through mitochondria allowing for fats and carbs to be used as fuel sources
- smaller motor neurons allowing for lower impulse on contraction
