#### History
Sashiko emerged during the Edo period (1603-1867) under the Tokugawa shogunate as a way for Farmers and Fishermen to mend clothing and reinforce garmets to add warmth during the cold Northern Japanese winters. Cotton came late to the north so the only way for most to get a hold of it was through tiny rags of fabric from southern tradesmen ([source](https://www.bbc.com/culture/article/20231013-the-300-year-old-japanese-method-of-upcyling)). If one could find a piece, they would connect those pieces to their clothing through a series of stitches. The resulting clothing was named *boro boro* (meaning 'tattered clothing or rags').
