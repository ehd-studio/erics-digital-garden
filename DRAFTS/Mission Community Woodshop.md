Mission Community Woodshop is a wood working studio based in inner mission of San Francisco. Run by two former teachers, Andrew and Daniel, the studio aims to teach the craft of woodworking and provide workspaces for artisans to utilize.

The studio approached me with the goal of creating new streams of revenue to expand on their current offering and support a residency program. We discussed building a fundraising program, digital assets to support outreach, updating their web platform and creating a blueprint for improving their digital footprint.

### Follow up
I shared the slideshow along with an estimate for the cost of everything I presented.

I provided an estimate of what the video would cost based on a 1:30 minute video without sound. I gave options to customize if they wanted to add sound, extend the timing or change the setup. I also added the cost of adding the video to their current site.

The two founders had questions regarding live scenarios where video is implemented and broken up across a website. I gave a Wix specific example of a video banner in order to show how our work could be implemented into their current site. There were also other examples I provided showing how other websites have integrated an introductory video into the banner of a website.
