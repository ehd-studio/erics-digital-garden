### Syllabus
Individual term project. Over the course of the semester, each student will pursue a
term project built around their own individual career interests:
- In the first few classes of the semester, students will identify a media-related
career path or topic that they are personally interested in exploring. For example:
	- Someone further interested in academia might identify a specific
	topic they may want to explore more.
	- Someone interested in the business side of media may identify a
	role, company or industry they want to learn more about.
	- An aspiring entrepreneur or creative may identify a business they
may want to start or pursue.
- From there, each student, in ongoing dialogue with me, will identify a specific
central question to explore which will require a more in-depth analysis.
- In our 6th class, each student will present their term project proposals.
- As the semester continues and we cover different topics in class every week,
students will pursue their research and analysis, incorporating these class
concepts. For example, after we cover market structures or financial analysis,
students will tie those concepts into their own analysis.
- Students will share their personal analysis updates and reflections to me in
weekly email assignments. We will also have regular in-class roundtable
updates. For example, the week after we cover tech disruption, we may go
around and discuss how everyone’s topic is affected by tech disruption.
- In the last two classes, each student will present the conclusion of their term
project in an abbreviated deck.
- By the last class, students will also submit a finished, comprehensive deck with
their full analysis and conclusions. Ultimately their analysis will include their
central question and why it was personally important to them, an industry
overview, market analysis, a cross-industry comparison, financial analysis and
conclusion.
