### Reaching Out
*Post Talk/Presentation*
```
Hey [Panel Member],

I found your discussion over [topic of the speech] incredibly interesting and relevant to the work I'm doing now.

Do you have a couple minutes over phone or over coffee to discuss what you were talking about?

Thank you in advance for your consideration!

Regards,
Eric
```
### Pre-Interview Research
- Know the person you're talking to
	- LinkedIn, Youtube talks, published works, panels, speeches, press releases
- Articulate the reason you want to discuss your topic with them
- Ask about their experiences 

### The Follow Up
- Email them thanking them!
	- Immediately post talk
	- Email down the road to discuss how advice/ideas resonated to what you're doing now

*Immediate Follow Up Template*
```
Hey [Interviewee Name],

Thank you again for taking the time to discuss things with me.

Hope to connect again in the near future.

Regards,
Eric
```
